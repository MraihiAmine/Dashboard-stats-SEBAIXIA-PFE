History and Identity

Lapeyre is a French company specialized in sustainable home improvement, offering a wide range of products: joinery (doors, windows, stairs), kitchens, bathrooms, storage solutions, and more.

It was founded in 1931 by Martial Lapeyre and quickly became known for its fixed-price catalog and focus on accessible quality.

Over time, the company changed ownership several times: acquired by Poliet in 1975, then by Saint-Gobain in 1996, and later sold to the German group Mutares in 2021.

Today, Lapeyre operates 9 production sites and around 130 stores across France, with additional factories and points of sale internationally.
